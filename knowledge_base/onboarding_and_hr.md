# Onboarding & HR Standards

## Coordination

- Any onboarding or HR-related task requires direct coordination with the HR department — it is never assigned solely to an engineering team, even if the task involves setting up technical accounts or access.
- Onboarding documents for new interns and employees are prepared by the HR team; engineering teams contribute technical checklists but HR owns the document itself.

## Task shape

- Onboarding tasks are tracked as a checklist-style task per new hire, not one giant recurring task shared across multiple hires.
- Engineering-specific onboarding steps (repo access, local environment setup, first PR) can be split into a sub-task owned by the relevant engineering team, linked back to the main HR onboarding task.

## Priority

- Onboarding tasks default to `Priority: Low` unless there is a hard start date within the next few days, in which case they become `Priority: Medium`.
